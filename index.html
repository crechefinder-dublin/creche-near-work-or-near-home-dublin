Choosing a Creche Near Work or Near Home in Dublin

Should your creche be near your workplace or near home? Weigh up commuting, sick days, flexibility, friendships and the move to school before you decide.

Location is one of the most practical decisions in choosing a creche. With many Dublin parents commuting into the city or across it, a common question is whether to choose a creche close to home or close to work.

Both options can work well. The best choice depends on your commute, your job, who does drop-off and collection, and your plans for the years ahead. This guide sets out the main points to weigh up.

At a glance

Near home

  • Local friendships that can continue into school
  • Easy for either parent or a relative to collect
  • No need to bring a child on a long commute
  • Simpler on days you work from home
  • Often feeds into local primary schools

Near work

  • Close by if your child is unwell during the day
  • More time together on the journey
  • Pick-up fits naturally into the working day
  • Possible to visit at lunchtime if the creche allows
  • Workplace creches may be available in some sectors
01

The daily commute

Think honestly about the journey. Bringing a baby or toddler on a busy train, bus or car journey twice a day can be tiring for everyone, especially in winter. On the other hand, some parents value that time together and find it a calm start and end to the day. Consider traffic, public transport reliability and what happens on days of disruption.

02

When your child is sick

Creches have illness policies and will call you to collect a child who becomes unwell. If you work close to the creche you can get there quickly. If the creche is near home and you work in the city centre, you will need someone nearby who can step in, or a workplace that understands you may need to leave suddenly.

  • Who could collect at short notice if you cannot?
  • How long would it take you to reach the creche from work?
  • Does your employer offer flexibility for family emergencies?
03

Working from home and changing jobs

Hybrid working has changed this decision for many families. If you work from home several days a week, a creche near work means commuting in on days you would otherwise stay at home. Think too about job security and future moves: a creche near home stays convenient even if your workplace changes.

04

Friendships and starting school

Children at a local creche often make friends who live nearby and may go on to the same primary school. These friendships can make the move to school feel more familiar. A creche near work may draw children from a wide area, so friendships may be harder to continue outside creche hours.

05

Two parents, two workplaces

If both parents work, look at the whole picture. Who will do most drop-offs and collections? Does one of you have more flexible hours? Sometimes a creche halfway between home and one workplace, or near a transport hub, is the most workable option.

06

Questions to ask yourselves

  • What does a realistic morning look like on a wet Monday?
  • What happens when one of us is travelling for work?
  • Will we use the same creche once a sibling arrives?
  • How long do we expect to stay in our current home and jobs?
  • Is there an opening-hours match with our working day and commute?
07

Thinking ahead to school age

Your child will be in creche for only a few years before primary school. When choosing a location, think about where after-school care will be needed later. A creche near home may also offer a school-age service with collection from local schools, which can make the next transition much simpler. If you choose a creche near work, plan ahead for how school-age care will work once your child starts school near home.

!

Tip: Do a trial run

Before you commit, do the full journey at the real times: from home to creche to work, and back again at collection time. Ten minutes on a map can look very different at 8am in Dublin traffic.

There is no single right answer. Choose the location that makes everyday life calmer for your family, and review it as your circumstances change.
